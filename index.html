Success Story: Andes Fresh Exports

A company profile

Ten years ago, Andes Fresh Exports was a small family business selling fruit only in the local market. Today, the company exports avocados and blueberries to more than fifteen countries, including Germany, South Korea, and the United Arab Emirates. The founder, now the general manager, explains that building trust with international buyers has always been demanding, but the results have been worth the effort. Last year, the company opened a new packing facility to meet growing demand from Asian markets. Employees have received training in quality control and international shipping documentation, skills that are essential for maintaining strong client relationships. However, the company has also faced challenges: strict customs regulations in some countries and rising transportation costs have slowed expansion at times. Despite these obstacles, the management team remains confident about future growth, especially in emerging markets in the Middle East. The company's next goal is to open a distribution office in Dubai within the next two years.

III. Writing

12 points
Write a cover letter for the job posting below. Follow the guiding questions to organize your ideas.
Escribe una carta de presentación para la oferta de trabajo a continuación. Usa las preguntas guía para organizar tus ideas.

NORTHFIELD TRADING CO.

is looking for a Junior Import-Export Assistant to join our logistics team. We need someone organized, reliable, and eager to learn international trade operations.

  1. Why are you the best candidate for the position? (construct: simple present — "I am...", "I work well with...")
  2. Include information about your education: What and where did you study? (construct: simple past — "I studied... at...")
  3. Remember your recent jobs. What have you done/learned there? (construct: simple past + present perfect — "I worked as...", "I have learned...")
  4. Name one achievement that you have completed and one that you haven't completed yet. (construct: present perfect — "I have achieved...", "I haven't... yet")
  5. Make predictions about your professional life 5 years from now. (construct: future — "I will...", "I am going to...", "I am ...ing")

Important: Include at least 8 vocabulary words from the vocabulary studied in class.

Tip for students: try to use at least one sentence per question above, that naturally builds a well-organized letter.
